# Joint Accounts and Authorized Users

Effective ${pol_joint_effective_date}. This policy explains how joint accounts work and what each owner can see and do.

## Ownership

A joint account has two or more owners. Each owner has full rights to the account. Either owner can deposit, withdraw, transfer, and set up scheduled payments, and each owner is responsible for any fees or overdrafts on the account.

## Deposits and payments

A deposit made by any owner is credited to the joint account and follows the normal funds availability schedule. A scheduled payment set up by any owner runs from the joint account on its scheduled date.

## Privacy between account holders

Owning a joint account does not give access to the other owner's individual accounts. In online banking, the mobile app, and our digital assistant, each customer sees only the accounts they hold. To see another person's individual account, that person must add you as an owner or authorized user.

## Authorized users

An authorized user can make deposits and view activity on an account but does not own it. Only an owner can add or remove an authorized user.

## Removing a joint owner

All owners must agree in writing to remove an owner from a joint account. An owner can close their own access at any time, but the account stays open for the remaining owners.
