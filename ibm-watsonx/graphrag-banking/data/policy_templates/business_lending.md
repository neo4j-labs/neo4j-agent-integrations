# Business Lending and Approval Authority

Effective ${pol_lend_effective_date}. This policy is for bank staff. It sets the size limit for business lines of credit and who may approve them.

## Business line of credit size

Rule code: LEND-BUSLINE-REV

A business line of credit can be at most ${lend_busline_rev_percent} percent of the business's verified annual revenue. Verified revenue comes from the business's deposit history and tax returns. Stated revenue on the application is a starting point only. Deposits that the fraud red flags policy excludes do not count as verified revenue.

## Relationship manager approval limit

Rule code: AUTH-RM-LIMIT

A relationship manager can approve business credit up to ${auth_rm_limit_amount} for clients in their own book. Larger requests go to the credit committee. No one may approve an application while a fraud referral on it is open.
