# Wire Transfer Policy

Effective ${pol_wire_effective_date}. This policy explains how outgoing and incoming wires are processed, what each wire status means, and what can happen to a wire after you send it.

## Sending a wire

You can send a domestic wire in online banking, in the mobile app, or at a branch. You need the beneficiary's name, bank routing number, and account number. The amount is reserved from your available balance as soon as you submit the wire.

## Cutoff times and processing

Wires submitted on a business day before 4:00 PM Eastern Time are usually sent the same day. Wires submitted later are sent the next business day. Some wires are reviewed before they are sent, as described in Payment Screening and Compliance Review.

## Wire status

- **Pending:** the wire has been submitted and is waiting to be sent.
- **Held:** the wire is on hold for a review before it is sent. A held wire has not failed. Most reviews finish within one business day, and the wire is then sent or you are contacted.
- **Posted:** the wire has been sent.
- **Failed:** the wire could not be sent, for example because the beneficiary details were incomplete. The reserved funds are released.
- **Returned:** the wire was sent, but the receiving bank sent it back. The funds are credited back to your account.

## Wire fees

Outgoing wires cost ${fee_wire_out_amount} and incoming wires cost ${fee_wire_in_amount}. A returned wire is charged ${fee_wire_return_amount}. ${chk_prem_name} waives all three. The full terms are in the Fee Schedule.

## Cancelling a wire

You can cancel a wire while it is pending or held by calling customer support. A wire cannot be cancelled after it is posted.
