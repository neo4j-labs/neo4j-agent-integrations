# Fraud Referrals and Suspicious Activity

Effective ${pol_sar_effective_date}. This policy is for bank staff. It explains when to refer an application to the fraud team and what staff may say to the client while a referral is open.

## Fraud referral before a decision

Rule code: FRAUD-REFERRAL

An application that matches ${fraud_referral_min_red_flags} or more red flags must be referred to the fraud team before any credit decision. Staff must not approve or decline the application while the referral is open. The fraud team reviews the evidence and tells the relationship manager when the application can move forward.

## No tipping off

Rule code: AML-NO-TIPOFF

Staff must not tell a client, or anyone acting for a client, that a fraud referral or a suspicious activity report exists or is being considered. This applies to calls, emails, letters, and meetings. Staff must not hint at a referral or suggest the client change their application to avoid one.

## What staff may say to the client

When a client asks about a delayed application, staff may say: "Your application is still in review. Reviews can take longer when we need to verify more information. We will contact you when the review is complete." Staff may ask the client for documents that any applicant could be asked for, such as tax returns or bank statements. Staff must not give a reason for the delay that mentions fraud, a referral, or a review by another team.
