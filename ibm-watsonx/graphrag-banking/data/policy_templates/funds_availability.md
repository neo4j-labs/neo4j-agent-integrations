# Funds Availability and Check Holds

Effective ${pol_funds_effective_date}. This policy explains when deposited funds become available to spend. Until funds are available, they are not counted in your available balance.

## Business days and cutoff times

A business day is Monday through Friday, except federal holidays. Deposits made after the cutoff time, or on a day that is not a business day, are treated as received on the next business day. The cutoff for mobile, ATM, and branch deposits is 8:00 PM Eastern Time.

## Check deposits

Rule code: HOLD-CHECK-NBD

Checks deposited on a business day before the cutoff are available ${hold_check_nbd_hold_days} business day after the day of deposit, which is the next business day. For example, a check deposited on a Thursday morning is available on Friday. While the deposit is pending it shows in your account activity, but it is not part of your available balance.

## Large deposits

Rule code: HOLD-CHECK-LARGE

When check deposits on one business day total more than ${hold_check_large_threshold}, we may hold the amount above ${hold_check_large_threshold} for up to ${hold_check_large_hold_days} business days. The first ${hold_check_large_threshold} follows the normal check deposit schedule. We will tell you if a longer hold applies and when the funds will be available.

## Electronic deposits

Direct deposits, other ACH credits, and incoming wires are available on the day we receive them.

## Returned deposits

If a deposited check is returned unpaid after the funds are available, we reverse the deposit and you are responsible for the amount. The returned deposited item fee is in the Fee Schedule.
