# Payment Screening and Compliance Review

Effective ${pol_screen_effective_date}. We screen payments to protect customers from fraud and to meet our legal obligations. This policy explains which payments are reviewed and what customers can expect.

## Why payments are reviewed

Some payments are held briefly for review before they are sent. A review is a standard step. It does not mean the payment has failed or that the customer has done anything wrong.

## New payee wire review

Rule code: WIRE-REVIEW-01

The first outgoing wire to a new payee is held for a standard review when it is over ${wire_review_01_threshold}. Reviews usually finish within one business day. When the review is complete, the wire is sent and no further action is needed. A wire held for this review has not failed, and it is not charged a returned-wire fee.

## Sanctions screening

Rule code: SANCTIONS-SCREEN-01

Every wire and outgoing ACH payment is screened against government sanctions lists. If the payee or sender is a potential match, the payment is held until the match is cleared. Most potential matches are false positives and clear within two business days.

## High-value activity review

Rule code: AML-VELOCITY-01

When outgoing payments from one account total more than ${aml_velocity_01_threshold} within 24 hours, the account activity is reviewed. Payments may be held during the review.

## What customers can expect

We may contact you to confirm a payment. We will never ask for your password or a one-time passcode. You can check a payment's status in online banking or by asking customer support.
