# Account Products and Tiers

Effective ${pol_products_effective_date}. This policy describes the deposit accounts we offer and the main terms of each. Fees are listed in the Fee Schedule. Overdraft terms are in the Overdraft Policy.

## ${chk_std_name}

${chk_std_name} is our everyday checking account. The monthly maintenance fee is ${chk_std_monthly_fee}. It is waived in any statement period with direct deposits totaling $$500 or more. Standard fees apply to wires, overdrafts, and Overdraft Protection transfers. ${chk_std_name} can be opened as an individual or a joint account.

## ${chk_prem_name}

${chk_prem_name} is our relationship checking account. The monthly maintenance fee is ${chk_prem_monthly_fee}. It is waived when combined deposit balances are $$15,000 or more. ${chk_prem_name} includes:

- No fee for outgoing or incoming wires.
- No returned-wire fee.
- No overdraft fees and no Overdraft Protection transfer fees.

These benefits apply only to the ${chk_prem_name} account itself. They do not extend to other accounts the same customer holds.

## ${sav_std_name}

${sav_std_name} is an interest-bearing savings account. The monthly maintenance fee is ${sav_std_monthly_fee}. It is waived when the daily balance stays at $$300 or more. A ${sav_std_name} account can be linked to a checking account for Overdraft Protection.

## Changing products

You can change from ${chk_std_name} to ${chk_prem_name}, or back, at any time in online banking or at a branch. The new terms apply from the next statement period. Fees charged before the change are not refunded.
