# Account Products and Tiers

Effective January 1, 2026. This policy describes the deposit accounts we offer and the main terms of each. Fees are listed in the Fee Schedule. Overdraft terms are in the Overdraft Policy.

## Standard Checking

Standard Checking is our everyday checking account. The monthly maintenance fee is $12. It is waived in any statement period with direct deposits totaling $500 or more. Standard fees apply to wires, overdrafts, and Overdraft Protection transfers. Standard Checking can be opened as an individual or a joint account.

## Premium Checking

Premium Checking is our relationship checking account. The monthly maintenance fee is $25. It is waived when combined deposit balances are $15,000 or more. Premium Checking includes:

- No fee for outgoing or incoming wires.
- No returned-wire fee.
- No overdraft fees and no Overdraft Protection transfer fees.

These benefits apply only to the Premium Checking account itself. They do not extend to other accounts the same customer holds.

## Standard Savings

Standard Savings is an interest-bearing savings account. The monthly maintenance fee is $5. It is waived when the daily balance stays at $300 or more. A Standard Savings account can be linked to a checking account for Overdraft Protection.

## Changing products

You can change from Standard Checking to Premium Checking, or back, at any time in online banking or at a branch. The new terms apply from the next statement period. Fees charged before the change are not refunded.
