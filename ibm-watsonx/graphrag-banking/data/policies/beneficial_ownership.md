# Beneficial Ownership

Effective January 1, 2026. This policy is for bank staff. It explains who counts as the real owner of a business client, and how staff find them before extending credit.

## Why beneficial owners matter

A business can be owned by other companies. The people at the top of those chains control the business, even when their names are not on the application. Staff must know who they are before the bank lends to the business.

## Beneficial owner threshold

Rule code: KYC-UBO-25

Anyone with an effective ownership share of 25 percent or more is a beneficial owner of the business. To find a person's effective share, follow each ownership chain from the person down to the business and multiply the shares along the way. If a person reaches the business through more than one chain, add the chains together. For example, a person who owns 80 percent of a holding company that owns 70 percent of the business has an effective share of 56 percent.

## What staff must check

Staff must identify every beneficial owner before a credit decision. Staff must check each beneficial owner's history with the bank, including any loans through other businesses they own. An owner who appears only through a holding company is still a beneficial owner.
