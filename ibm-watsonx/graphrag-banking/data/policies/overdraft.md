# Overdraft Policy

Effective January 1, 2026. This policy explains what happens when a payment would take your checking account below zero, and how to avoid overdraft fees.

## What an overdraft is

An overdraft happens when we pay an item, such as a card purchase or an ACH payment, that is larger than your available balance. Your account balance becomes negative until you deposit enough to cover it.

## Overdraft fee

Rule code: FEE-OVERDRAFT

On Standard Checking, we charge an overdraft fee of $29 for each item we pay that overdraws the account. We do not charge an overdraft fee if the account is overdrawn by $50 or less after the item is paid.

## Overdraft Protection

Rule code: FEE-ODP-TRANSFER

Overdraft Protection links a savings account to your checking account. If an item would overdraw your checking account, we automatically transfer money from the linked savings account to cover it, so no overdraft fee is charged. Each Overdraft Protection transfer costs $10. An owner of the checking account must also own the linked savings account. You can set it up in online banking or at a branch.

## Avoiding overdraft fees

- Keep track of your available balance, including pending payments.
- Turn on low-balance alerts in online banking.
- Link a savings account with Overdraft Protection.
- Make sure deposits are available before large scheduled payments run. See Funds Availability and Check Holds.

## Premium Checking

Premium Checking accounts are not charged overdraft fees or Overdraft Protection transfer fees. This applies only to the Premium Checking account, not to other accounts the customer holds.
