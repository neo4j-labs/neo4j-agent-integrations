# Fee Schedule

Effective January 1, 2026. This schedule lists the fees for deposit accounts. A fee is charged only on products it applies to, and some products waive it. The product terms are in Account Products and Tiers.

## Monthly maintenance fees

- Standard Checking: $12 per month.
- Premium Checking: $25 per month.
- Standard Savings: $5 per month.

Each product's waiver conditions are in Account Products and Tiers.

## Wire transfer fees

Rule code: FEE-WIRE-OUT
Rule code: FEE-WIRE-IN

- Outgoing domestic wire: $30 per wire.
- Incoming wire: $15 per wire.

The outgoing wire fee is charged when the wire is sent. Premium Checking accounts are not charged either wire fee.

## Returned wire fee

Rule code: FEE-WIRE-RETURN

When the receiving bank returns an outgoing wire, for example because the beneficiary account number is wrong or the account is closed, we charge a returned-wire fee of $35. The returned funds are credited back to your account.

The returned-wire fee applies only to wires that were sent and then returned. It is not charged for a wire that is held for review or cancelled before it is sent. Premium Checking accounts are not charged the returned-wire fee.

## Returned deposited item fee

Rule code: FEE-DEPOSIT-RETURN

If a check you deposit is returned unpaid by the paying bank, we charge $12 and reverse the deposit. This fee applies to all deposit accounts.

## Overdraft fees

Overdraft fees and Overdraft Protection transfer fees are described in the Overdraft Policy.
