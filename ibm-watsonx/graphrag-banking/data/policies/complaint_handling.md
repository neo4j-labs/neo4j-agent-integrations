# Complaint Handling

Effective January 1, 2026. This policy explains how to raise a complaint and how we respond.

## How to file a complaint

You can file a complaint in online banking, by calling customer support, or at any branch. Please include the account, the date, and what happened.

## Response times

We acknowledge complaints within two business days and aim to resolve them within fifteen business days. If we need more time, we will tell you why and when to expect an answer.

## Fee disputes and refunds

If you think a fee was charged in error, tell us within 60 days of the statement that shows it. If the fee does not match the Fee Schedule or the terms of your product, we refund it.

## Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can ask for the complaint to be reviewed by a senior complaints specialist. You can also contact your banking regulator.
